The platform

Every HR workflow, under one roof.

Each module stands on its own, but they share the same employee record, approval engine and permission model — so nothing drifts out of sync.

Employee lifecycle

Invitations, onboarding, org structure, custom fields and status history — from first day to exit, with a full activity log.

Leave & attendance

Configurable leave types, entitlements and holiday calendars, plus attendance records with a built-in correction workflow.

Payroll & compliance

Multi-stage payroll runs with PAYE, Pension and NHF calculated automatically, plus payslips, loans and payment exports.

Document compliance

Track required documents by role or department, with expiry reminders, approval workflows and request-a-signature replies.

Service desk & assets

Ticket routing by category and SLA, plus an asset register that tracks assignment, condition and incident history.

Approvals & automation

One configurable approval engine behind every workflow, plus an operations centre that automates routine reminders.

Payroll

Payroll that already understands Nigerian statutory law.

Pension contributions, PAYE brackets and NHF are calculated for every run — not bolted on afterward. Mid-period joiners, leavers and salary changes are prorated automatically.
